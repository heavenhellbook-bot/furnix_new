### What provides machining positions and clearances?

**Blum is the clearest route to a cabinet-level machining export.** Its Product Configurator produces parts lists, CAD data, and planning information; saved configurations can be reused as templates in “My library.” For a detailed configuration, its **BXF (Blum Exchange Format)** export includes hardware information and wood-part manufacturing information, specifically cutting dimensions and drilling positions. Blum says BXF can be imported into CAD software or used with MINIPRESS P + EASYSTICK. ([Product Configurator](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/onlineproductconfigurator/); [Blum FAQ: BXF and CAD data](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/faq/); [EASYSTICK: BXF workflow](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/assembly-devices/easystick/program/))

Blum’s **Cabinet Configurator** is aimed at planning complete cabinets, with collision checking and planning results intended to support the process from design through manufacturing. It is a useful source for checking configurations and clearances, but its output should not be confused with an unrestricted public API. ([Cabinet Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/cabinet-configurator/))

**Hettich provides two levels of CAD information.** Its Hettich CAD page says manufacturer-backed drawings—including drilling data and master-data logic for many fittings—are integrated in partner CAD/CAM material libraries. Hettich’s eShop offers direct CAD downloads in more than 80 formats, including 2D/3D DWG/DXF, 3D previews and 2D sectional PDFs; Hettich lists position determination and drilling-point determination among the benefits. Those are useful product-level drawings and geometry, but the page does not say every downloadable format is a machine-ready drilling program. ([Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/hettich-cad); [Hettich eShop](https://shop.hettich.com/us_EN))

For parametric placement and manufacturing logic, Hettich describes partner libraries that embed fittings and their machining data in the partner’s software. For example, Hettich says the Pytha package can be inserted parametrically into a furniture opening with minimum and maximum limits observed; its TopSolid description references exact fitting positions and drilling information. ([Pytha data packages](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages/pytha); [TopSolid information](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages/topsolid))

### Physical templates and workshop aids

CAD data is not the only official source for transferring a hole pattern. Blum sells templates and assembly tools for its fittings, and its EASYSTICK/MINIPRESS workflow calculates drilling positions and sets machine stops. These are manufacturer-supported workshop routes, not general-purpose downloads of every drilling map. ([Blum templates and tools](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/assembly-devices/assembly-templates/overview/); [Blum EASYSTICK](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/assembly-devices/easystick/program/))

Hettich’s assembly-aids page covers drilling jigs and drawer-assembly aids, including AvanFit and ArciFit tools; it says drilling patterns can be transferred to the workpiece. This is a practical transfer/assembly route, distinct from a parametric CAD/CAM library. ([Hettich assembly aids](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/assembly-aids); [Hettich eShop](https://shop.hettich.com/us_EN))

### What is gated, and what is not?

- **Blum:** Blum states that full E-SERVICES access requires a free account; guest access is limited. The Product Database provides technical details, drawings and CAD files after access. BXF is created from a Product Configurator configuration; the public material reviewed describes an interactive workflow, not an open API or blanket right to redistribute BXF data. ([Blum services overview](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/overview/); [Product Database](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/productdatabase/); [FAQ](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/faq/))
- **Blum software partners:** Blum maintains integrations with CAD/CAM vendors, including Cabinet Vision, Microvellum, Mozaik and PYTHA. Its Cabinet Vision page specifically documents BXF import through the S2M Center. Access to those native libraries is through the relevant software product/integration; this should not be taken as a public right to copy Blum’s library into another application. ([Blum software partners](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/softwarepartners/); [CABINET VISION integration](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/softwarepartners/cabinet-vision/))
- **Hettich:** Individual CAD drawings are available through the Hettich eShop, while richer machining logic is described as part of partner software libraries. Certain software-specific packages, such as Pytha and imos, are published for download. Hettich’s download terms grant a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable right for business use **for selling Hettich products**, and prohibit other purposes and alteration. Get written permission before embedding, extracting, repackaging or redistributing those files in FURNIX. ([Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/hettich-cad); [Pytha packages](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages/pytha); [download terms, status July 2025](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/terms-of-use/terms-of-use-for-downloads))

**I found no official Hettich source in this research that documents a general-purpose, publicly exportable BXF-like cabinet/CAM file.** The documented Hettich path is direct product CAD downloads plus licensed or software-specific partner libraries. That is a finding about the sources reviewed, not proof that no private partner interface exists.

### Recommended legitimate route for FURNIX

1. **For immediate manual use:** register for Blum E-SERVICES and generate configurations/BXF through the official Product Configurator; obtain Hettich drawings through its eShop or the official data package for a software environment FURNIX actually uses.
2. **For an in-app library or automated CAM:** contact Blum and Hettich through their official service/CAD channels and request a written data or software-partner agreement. Specify the needed systems, country/market, data fields (part numbers, dimensions, clearances, hole coordinates, compatible components), refresh/update mechanism, and whether FURNIX will generate customer outputs or redistribute manufacturer data.
3. **Do not derive production maps from product images or reuse partner-library archives outside their licensed software context.** Use current, item-number-specific manufacturer data and verify the chosen runner, drawer system, front fixing, accessories, motion technology and cabinet dimensions together. The manufacturers’ pages are region-specific, and drawings/configurations can vary by product variant and market.

The Hettich **2025 Furniture Fittings and Applications Catalogue** is also an official reference for product selection and technical documentation, but the online catalogue is JavaScript-based; for production coordinates, use the relevant product-specific CAD/processing data as well. ([2025 catalogue](https://catalog.hettich.com/General/TuI_2025/en_DE/); [Hettich downloads/media library](https://www.hettich.com/en-us/services-1/downloads))
