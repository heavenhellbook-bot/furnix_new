# Official furniture-hardware compatibility data sources

**As of 1 November 2026.** The sources show a meaningful distinction between downloadable 3D geometry and data that can actually drive cabinet planning, machining, or compatibility checks. For a CAD product, the strongest starting points are Hettich and Blum, but in different ways: Hettich documents CAD-partner integrations with drilling and master-data logic, while Blum documents integrated configuration, parts lists, and production outputs. Salice and GRASS offer useful product-level technical information and gated or less clearly documented design resources. I could not verify Häfele’s current offering beyond its official CAD-data URL because its site returned access errors.

## What data can a furniture CAD product practically obtain?

### Takeaway
Official manufacturer sources offer product catalogs, downloadable CAD, technical drawings, and planning tools—but access and structure vary. The most actionable information is generally exposed through a manufacturer’s configurator, authenticated portal, or named CAD-software partnership, rather than as an open API or unrestricted data feed.

### Cited Findings
- **Blum CAD/CAM Data Service** advertises downloads for individual products and configured applications, including 3D CAD, 2D installation situations, 2D production drawings, complete CAD packages, and CAM machining macros. Its public page says exports are available in “usual CAD formats” and WOP formats, but does **not** name specific file extensions. [Blum CAD/CAM Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/industrial-production/cad-cam-dataservice/)
- **Blum Product Database** provides market-specific item overviews, technical features, descriptions, product drawings, images, and CAD downloads. It is an E-SERVICES application; the page says personal access data is available through a Blum contact partner. [Blum Product Database](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/product-database/)
- **Blum Product Data Service** is explicitly for software partners: Blum provides a complete product-data package for integration and regular updates. The public page describes the service but not an open API, a downloadable schema, or public licensing terms; it directs interested software providers to contact Blum. [Blum Product Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/productdata-service/)
- Blum’s **Cabinet Configurator** adds actionable cabinet-level output: collision checking, fitting selection, CAD export, parts lists, cutting lists, assembly overviews, manufacturing drawings, and BXF files. The page says BXF contains fitting information and manufacturing information for wooden parts, including cutting dimensions and drilling positions. [Blum Cabinet Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/cabinet-configurator/)
- Blum’s **CAD/CAM interface** page distinguishes partner-library data from simple models: partner software can integrate Blum’s fittings configurator, a BXF interface, or a fittings library; Blum describes collision-checked solutions with assembly data, parts lists, fittings data, and wooden parts. [Blum CAD/CAM interface](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/industrial-production/cad-cam-interface/)
- **Hettich CAD** says its CAD partners can provide original drawings, drilling data, and master-data logic directly in material libraries for many Hettich products. Hettich also describes a free CAD service, and says that for most articles it offers corresponding CAD data; for a missing article, users can email the article and desired format. The public page does not enumerate every format available across all partners. [Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad) · [Hettich cabinet-maker services](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/homepage/cabinet-makers)
- Hettich’s partner documentation includes **structured compatibility/manufacturing examples**, not just visual geometry: CAD+T’s Connection Scan automatically selects connectors, and the page says the complete Hettich connector database can be downloaded or obtained through iFurn. The same page lists supported hinge, drawer-system, runner, lift-fitting, and other product families for participating CAD systems; coverage differs by software partner. [Hettich CAD data packages](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages)
- Hettich’s public Downloads page describes a media library of catalogs, videos, animations, documentation, certificates, and other publications, with online viewing and direct downloads. It links separate download terms. [Hettich Downloads](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services-1/downloads)
- **GRASS**’s official Tec-Center says its download center includes assembly instructions, product catalogs, CAD data, and environmental certificates. The page does not specify CAD file formats or an API. [GRASS Downloads](https://www.grass.eu/en/tec-center/downloads/)
- GRASS’s **slides comparison** exposes useful product-selection attributes—load capacity, extension type, adjustment dimensions, synchronization, damping, opening systems, and drawer compatibility. This is structured product guidance, but it is not itself a downloadable compatibility database. [GRASS slides](https://www.grass.eu/en/products/slides/)
- **Salice**’s product pages provide human-readable engineering constraints. For example, its Silentia+ page specifies opening angle, suitable door thickness, cup diameter and depth, drilling distance, and mounting-plate compatibility. It also shows product/variant identifiers, but the page is not a machine-readable API. [Salice Silentia+](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/products/hinges/integrated-soft-close-mechanism/silentia-plus-series100-105-opening-standard-application)
- Salice’s account-based **Reserved Area** describes product configurators for exploring product variants, materials, and applications; outputs include technical documentation and 3D models. It also offers an iFurn library of 3D files. The page requires an account for the reserved services and does not specify the downloadable file formats or a public API. [Salice Reserved Area](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/reserved-area/home)

### Inferences
- A 3D model supports visualization and spatial placement, but it should not be treated as evidence of machinability or compatibility. Hettich’s explicit separation of drawing data, drilling data, and master-data logic—and Blum’s separate configurator, installation, and manufacturing outputs—supports maintaining distinct records for geometry, selection rules, and manufacturing instructions. ([Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad); [Blum Cabinet Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/cabinet-configurator/))
- I found **no publicly documented, unauthenticated API** on the official sources reviewed. The documented routes are portal downloads, manufacturer configurators, downloadable exports, direct requests to a manufacturer, and integrations through named CAD partners. This is a finding about the reviewed pages, not proof that no private API or partner feed exists.
- For each catalog record, keep at minimum: manufacturer and market, article/variant number, product family, compatible components, application constraints, drilling/installation data, source URL, source revision/date, and access/license status. This is a practical recommendation; manufacturers do not publish one common cross-brand schema.

### Gaps
- Public pages often say “usual CAD formats” or “3D files” without listing formats, fields, update cadence, or redistribution rights. Obtain those details from the manufacturer before implementation.
- No public source reviewed offered one complete cross-product machine-readable schema covering hinges, runners, drawer boxes, lifts, connectors, article numbers, machining, and compatibility.

## Manufacturer comparison: public information, planning tools, and access limits

### Takeaway
Hettich has the clearest documented route from CAD geometry to drilling/master data and connector selection. Blum has the strongest documented end-to-end planning-to-manufacturing workflow, but its integrated data package is specifically offered to software partners. Salice’s public pages are useful for rule discovery and its reserved configurators/iFurn library offer deeper resources. GRASS provides useful product-level comparisons and a download center, but public detail on formats and structured feeds is limited.

### Cited Findings
- **Blum — hinges, runners/pull-outs, drawer systems, lifts, and applications:** the Product Configurator supports fittings, cabinet applications, and motion technologies; it returns parts lists, CAD data, and planning information, and can send parts lists to selected distributor shops. Access is via Blum E-SERVICES, with guest and registered-service distinctions described on the page. [Blum Product Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/product-configurator/)
- **Blum — CAM and installation:** the manufacturer describes downloadable machining macros and WOP formats, as well as a separate EASY ASSEMBLY app with product assembly instructions, videos, drilling-position guidance, and adjustment help. [Blum CAD/CAM Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/industrial-production/cad-cam-dataservice/) · [EASY ASSEMBLY](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/assembly-adjustment/easy-assembly-app/)
- **Hettich — product families and international/CIS relevance:** Hettich’s CAD partner list includes broad furniture-CAD coverage and names Russian-language tools such as БАЗИС and bCAD, as well as Cadenas, iFurn, imos, and others. The Hettich site’s country selector includes CIS-region choices, and the company says it has subsidiaries in 24 countries. Regional product and portal availability should still be checked for each target market. [Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad) · [Hettich homepage](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/homepage)
- Hettich says users can export article lists in CSV from the eShop Notepad and use them with retailer shops; its cabinet-maker page also directs users to the official product catalog and Technical Assistant. [Hettich cabinet-maker services](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/homepage/cabinet-makers) · [Hettich catalog](https://catalog.hettich.com/General/TA_2025/en_DE/) · [Hettich Technical Assistant](https://hta.hettich.com/en-de/home)
- Hettich’s product pages illustrate why rules need more than geometry: its Sensys hinge page states features such as opening angle, temperature range, and shallow cup depth; its runner page describes a platform with common dimensions for wooden drawers and cabinet drill holes across runner choices. [Hettich Sensys](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/products/hinges/sensys) · [Hettich runner systems](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/products/runner-systems)
- **GRASS — slides, drawer systems, hinges, and lift/opening systems:** its slides page compares Dynapro and Dynaneo across capacity, extension, adjustment, synchronization, assembly, damping, opening-system compatibility, and compatible drawer types. Its Tiomos page describes applications and door-thickness ranges, but does not publish a machine-readable rules feed on the page. [GRASS slides](https://www.grass.eu/en/products/slides/) · [GRASS Tiomos](https://www.grass.eu/en/products/hinges/tiomos/) · [GRASS opening systems](https://www.grass.eu/en/products/opening-systems/)
- **Salice — hinges, runners/drawers, lifts:** product-category and product pages provide selection dimensions and visible compatibility information. The Reserved Area offers configurators, technical documentation, 3D models, and an iFurn library, but requires account access for reserved services. [Salice hinges](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/products/hinges) · [Salice runners and drawers](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/products/runners-and-drawers) · [Salice lift systems](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/products/lift-systems) · [Salice Reserved Area](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/reserved-area/home)
- **Häfele:** the official CAD-data URL is [Häfele CAD Data](https://www.hafele.com/us/en/info/service/cad-data/), but requests to Häfele pages returned HTTP 403 in this research run, so I could not confirm formats, authentication, integrations, or data fields from the page itself. Häfele’s official `robots.txt` says `search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference`; that is a site content signal, not a CAD-data license. [Häfele robots.txt](https://www.hafele.com/robots.txt)

### Inferences
- **Best first technical integration: Hettich.** Its official sources most explicitly connect CAD content to drilling data, master-data logic, automatic connector selection, and connector databases, and document partner coverage that includes CAD systems relevant to European/CIS workflows. Confirm the applicable data package and terms with Hettich or the specific CAD partner before ingesting it. ([Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad); [Hettich CAD data packages](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages))
- **Best strategic manufacturer integration: Blum.** Blum documents unusually complete planning and manufacturing outputs—collision checks, parts lists, BXF, drawings, and CAM macros—but its product-data package is described as a software-partner service requiring contact. Approach Blum early for written permission, feed structure, and update arrangements. ([Blum Product Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/productdata-service/); [Blum Cabinet Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/cabinet-configurator/))
- **Salice is a strong next candidate** where public rules are useful for a prototype and the product range fits, but treat the Reserved Area and iFurn as access-controlled resources and seek written permission for application/database reuse. ([Salice Reserved Area](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/reserved-area/home))
- **GRASS is a reasonable follow-on** for slide/drawer product rules and downloadable documentation, subject to confirming available data formats and licensing. Häfele should be evaluated through a direct account or representative, because this research could not verify the protected product/CAD pages. ([GRASS Downloads](https://www.grass.eu/en/tec-center/downloads/); [Häfele CAD Data](https://www.hafele.com/us/en/info/service/cad-data/))

### Gaps
- I could not independently verify Häfele portal contents or licensing because its official pages were blocked in the research tool.
- Public evidence reviewed does not establish whether Salice, GRASS, Häfele, Blum, or Hettich offer private partner APIs, commercial redistribution rights, bulk export terms, or guarantees about data retention and update cadence. Those require written manufacturer confirmation.

## Licensing, legal limits, and a realistic acquisition workflow

### Takeaway
Do not assume that a publicly viewable catalog, downloadable model, or CAD partner library may be copied into a commercial product database. The clearest published limits reviewed are Hettich’s download terms and Salice’s site-use terms; other manufacturers’ data rights must be confirmed directly. Build the acquisition process around authorization and provenance, not scraping.

### Cited Findings
- Hettich’s download terms state that downloaded content is protected by Hettich or third-party property rights. For the uses described there, users receive a simple, non-exclusive, non-transferable right that is revocable at any time, limited to business use for selling Hettich products (including their presentation/advertising). The terms prohibit editing, changing, or supplementing the content and require copyright notices to remain unchanged; they state German law and Bielefeld jurisdiction. Confirm whether those terms apply to a particular CAD package or whether separate terms govern it. [Hettich terms of use for downloads](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/terms-of-use/terms-of-use-for-downloads)
- Salice’s general conditions say the site’s technical drawings and downloadable content are protected and may not be reproduced, published, distributed, displayed, or modified without prior written authorization. The terms also warn that technical data may contain errors and may change or be removed. [Salice general conditions of use](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/general-conditions-of-use)
- Blum’s Product Data Service page invites software partners to contact Blum for its data package; the public sources reviewed do not themselves grant general redistribution rights for CAD or product data. [Blum Product Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/productdata-service/)
- Häfele’s official `robots.txt` provides content signals, including `ai-train=no` and `use=reference`. Do not interpret that as permission to ingest CAD assets or redistribute product data; the CAD page and any download-specific terms need direct review. [Häfele robots.txt](https://www.hafele.com/robots.txt)
- GRASS’s official download center advertises CAD data and other downloads, but the pages reviewed did not establish rights for using those downloads in a commercial cross-manufacturer catalog. [GRASS Downloads](https://www.grass.eu/en/tec-center/downloads/)

### Inferences
- A practical workflow is:
  1. **Select the initial scope** (for example hinges, runners, drawer systems, lifts, connectors) and target country/market; manufacturer pages and product availability may be market-specific. ([Blum Product Database](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/product-database/); [Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad))
  2. **Ask manufacturers for written data access terms** covering commercial use, storage, updates, derivatives/normalization, attribution, resale, and whether partner feeds or APIs exist. Blum explicitly points software providers to its partner data service; Hettich directs requests for missing CAD articles to its contact address. ([Blum Product Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/productdata-service/); [Hettich cabinet-maker services](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/homepage/cabinet-makers))
  3. **Acquire separate data layers**: visual geometry; product/article metadata; parametric configurations and compatibility; installation/drilling/manufacturing instructions. Treat the configurator’s selected bill of materials as authoritative only within its documented scope. ([Blum CAD/CAM Data Service](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/industrial-production/cad-cam-dataservice/); [Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad))
  4. **Preserve provenance and versioning** for every record, including article number, market, source system, retrieval date, revision, allowed use, and supersession status. Hettich’s terms explicitly reserve revocation and prohibit modification of covered downloads; Salice says content may change or be deleted. ([Hettich download terms](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/terms-of-use/terms-of-use-for-downloads); [Salice general conditions](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/general-conditions-of-use))
  5. **Validate rules against official planning/assembly tools** and real manufactured parts before making a design appear production-ready. Blum documents collision-checked planning and drilling-position/assembly guidance; Hettich documents drilling data and master-data logic in partner libraries. ([Blum Cabinet Configurator](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/cabinet-configurator/); [Blum EASY ASSEMBLY](https://www.blum.com/ie/en/services/assembly-adjustment/easy-assembly-app/); [Hettich CAD](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/services/hettich-cad))

### Gaps
- This is a source review, not legal advice. The exact license for any specific CAD model, feed, or partner library may be different from a general website-download policy.
