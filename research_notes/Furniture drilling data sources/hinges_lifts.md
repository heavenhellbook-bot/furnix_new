## Blum

- **Product Configurator:** [official page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/onlineproductconfigurator/). Blum says users can configure hardware and cabinet applications, then obtain parts lists, CAD data, and planning information. Its planning results include design drawings downloadable in 2D CAD formats, while configured applications have downloadable 3D CAD in common formats. Blum advertises a **free account** for the configurator. This is the best first stop for configuration-specific planning and drawings; it is not merely a generic hardware library.
- **2D views and sections:** Blum’s [US home page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/) announces a download option for 2D views and sectional drawings from the Product Configurator for import into 2D CAD. This supports acquisition of actual drawings, but the page does not itself specify a commercial redistribution license.
- **Product Database:** [official page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/productdatabase/). A **free E-SERVICES account** unlocks product-specific technical attributes, CAD files, and planning and assembly drawings. Blum describes searching by item number, product number, or description and selecting available CAD formats. If a product has no CAD item, Blum directs users to contact its e-services team. This is useful for article-level drawings and geometry, but availability varies by item.
- **Assembly reference:** The [EASY ASSEMBLY app page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/easyassemblyapp/) says the free iOS/Android app provides step-by-step assembly and installation instructions and videos. It is an installer reference, not a documented structured machining-data export.
- **Catalog and product families:** The [online catalogue/technical manual](https://publications.blum.com/2026/catalogue/en/) is a public technical reference; its page identifies the catalogue as the **2027/2028** edition despite the URL’s `2026` segment. Blum’s [CLIP top page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/hingesystems/clip-top/programme/) shows that hinge applications vary by door/application and mounting-plate choice. Its [AVENTOS overview](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/liftsystems/aventos/overview/) describes multiple lift families and mounting options. Treat their product drawings/configurations as the source for actual coordinates—not one assumed pattern for all hinges or lifts.
- **CAD/CAM integrations:** Blum’s [software-partners page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/softwarepartners/) says supported design/manufacturing platforms contain manufacturer-backed libraries and lists partners including Cabinet Vision, Microvellum, Mozaik, PYTHA, and others. The [Microvellum page](https://www.blum.com/us/en/services/e-services/softwarepartners/microvellum/) documents supported Blum families. This verifies established integrations, not an open FURNIX API or blanket right to republish Blum data.

**Commercial-use note:** The public pages establish free-account access and intended planning/CAD workflows, but I did not find a public grant allowing FURNIX to copy, convert, bundle, or redistribute Blum geometry or drilling data in a commercial app. Treat that permission as unverified and ask Blum for written terms before embedding or rehosting data.

## Hettich

- **Hettich CAD overview:** [official CAD page](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/services/hettich-cad). Hettich says its CAD partners can provide original drawings—including **drilling data and master-data logic**—in their material libraries for many Hettich fittings. The page lists partners such as AutoCAD, Cadenas/PartCommunity, iFurn, Microvellum, Mozaik, PYTHA, SketchUp, TopSolid, and others. It also links an [official 2026 CAD-partner availability overview PDF](https://hett.b-cdn.net/fileadmin/Hettich_International/Service/Overview_CAD-Partner-2026_EN.pdf).
- **Direct CAD and installation-drawing downloads:** [Hettich CAD Downloads](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/services/hettich-cad/cad-downloads) offers downloadable product-family packages. Examples on the page include 2D Sensys application solutions and 2D TopLine L application solutions, plus sliding-door-system drawing packages in DWG/DXF, STEP, SAT, and IGES. These are actual fitting/application files, not a universal hole-pattern convention.
- **eShop CAD files:** The CAD overview says Hettich eShop provides more than 80 CAD formats, direct 2D/3D DWG/DXF downloads, more than 15,000 product data items, 3D previews, component assembly, and 2D sectional PDF drawings; it also identifies collision checks, dimension comparisons, position determination, and drilling-point determination as uses. Start at the [Hettich eShop](https://shop.hettich.com/de_EN/) and search by product/article; access or sign-in requirements may depend on the item or market.
- **Partner libraries:** [Hettich CAD data packages](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages) links partner-specific libraries. For example, the [PYTHA package page](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/services/hettich-cad/cad-data-packages/pytha) lists product libraries for Sensys, Intermat, Veosys, and sliding systems including TopLine L/XL. Hettich describes those PYTHA fittings as parametric data inserted into corresponding furniture openings with minimum/maximum limits observed; sliding-door examples can include whole cabinets with configurable dimensions.
- **Product and assembly references:** The [Sensys page](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/products/hinges/sensys) has product information, downloads, and service/support sections; the [TopLine L page](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/products/sliding-door-systems/topline-l) has installation and support sections. Hettich’s [assembly-aids page](https://www.hettich.com/en-de/services/assembly-aids) describes drilling jigs and aids for fittings, hinges, and mounting plates. These are useful installation cross-checks, not substitutes for the exact article’s machining data.
- **Use rights:** Hettich’s [download terms](https://www.hettich.com/en-ie/terms-of-use/terms-of-use-for-downloads), marked July 2025, say downloadable content is protected and grant a simple, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable right for use in business operations **for selling Hettich products**, including presentation/advertising. They prohibit other purposes and prohibit editing, changing, or supplementing the content. Do not assume those terms authorize incorporating or adapting drawings/data into FURNIX; obtain explicit permission for that use.

## Salice (useful secondary source)

- **Product-specific technical help:** Salice’s [Technical Services page](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/company/technical-services) says a customer can submit a description of a hinge application and a simple drawing; Salice’s CAD specialists respond with a technical drawing identifying the appropriate hinge and drilling distances. This is a practical route for edge cases or when a public drawing is unclear.
- **Public references:** [Catalogues](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/download/catalogues) and [Assembly Instructions](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/download/assembly-instructions) organize materials by product groups, including hinges, lift/flap systems, and sliding systems. The assembly page includes product-specific references such as Silentia+, Tilt, EvoLift, Wind, Slider, and Exedra. These are useful installation references; I did not verify a general public CAD/CAM API or a downloadable, structured hole-data library there.
- **Use rights:** Salice’s [General Conditions of Use](https://www.salice.com/ww/en/company/general-conditions-of-use) say technical drawings and downloadable content may not be reproduced, distributed, displayed, or modified without prior written authorization; it also restricts linking/framing without written authorization. FURNIX should obtain permission before integrating or republishing Salice content.

## Product-specific patterns versus conventions

The manufacturer sources point to article-level/configuration-level machining data: Hettich expressly identifies drilling data and drilling-point determination; Blum provides configuration results and article-specific drawings/CAD. The pattern depends on the selected fitting, variant, mounting plate, application, and cabinet/front geometry. A generic hole grid or a familiar hinge-cup convention is not a complete or safe substitute for the applicable manufacturer drawing—especially for plate holes, lift-system mounting positions, and sliding-door tracks.

For FURNIX, model machining features with provenance rather than storing an unlabeled “hinge pattern”: article/variant and revision; part datum and face; hole coordinates; diameter and depth; blind/through/counterbore type; edge references; fitting and mounting-plate IDs; applicable material/thickness and configuration; and source file/date. Validate each imported pattern against the manufacturer’s installation drawing before using it for CNC output.

1. **Blum pilot:** Register a free E-SERVICES account. In the Product Configurator, generate one real hinge and one lift configuration and export the available planning drawings, 2D views/sections, and CAD. Use the Product Database with exact article numbers to check for corresponding assembly/planning drawings and CAD.
2. **Hettich pilot:** Download the official CAD packages for one Sensys configuration and one TopLine family. In the eShop, search matching article numbers and compare available 2D/3D formats and sectional PDFs. Check the 2026 partner-availability PDF for coverage in FURNIX’s intended CAD/CAM workflow.
3. **Salice validation:** Send Technical Services a representative cabinet/front drawing and request the exact article, drilling distances, assembly drawing/CAD format, and written permission for FURNIX’s intended display and machining uses.
4. **Before integration:** Ask each manufacturer whether FURNIX may store, transform, cache, display, and distribute the data; generate machining/CNC outputs from it; and expose it to commercial users. Confirm attribution, update cadence, supported regions/products, liability, and any fees. Public download or a free configurator account alone does not establish those rights.
